## 04 / Engineering Philosophy

I believe strong software engineering goes beyond choosing frameworks or writing code that works once.

It requires understanding the underlying problem, making thoughtful technical decisions, and delivering solutions that remain useful as requirements evolve.

My approach follows a few principles:

**Understand the problem.**  
Identify user needs, constraints, and the consequences of technical decisions.

**Design intentionally.**  
Choose architectures and abstractions that match actual requirements.

**Build incrementally.**  
Break complex systems into manageable, verifiable components.

**Measure and improve.**  
Use testing, debugging, feedback, and observation to refine implementations.

**Document decisions.**  
Make the reasoning behind a system understandable to other engineers.

> Good engineering makes complicated problems manageable and useful solutions dependable.
